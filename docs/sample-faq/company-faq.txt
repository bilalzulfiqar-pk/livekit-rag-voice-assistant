LiveKit RAG Voice Assistant Sample FAQ

Q: What services do you offer?
A: We build LiveKit-based AI voice assistants, document-grounded RAG backends, FastAPI services, and custom frontend integrations for realtime AI experiences.

Q: Do you support uploaded knowledge-base documents?
A: Yes. The backend can ingest plain text, PDF, and DOCX files, generate embeddings, and answer grounded questions from the uploaded content.

Q: Can the assistant answer normal general questions too?
A: Yes. If a question is not about your uploaded documents or company knowledge, the voice agent can still answer normally with its base language model.

Q: How does weather support work in this demo?
A: The voice agent can call a weather tool for current weather in a named city and respond with a short voice-friendly answer.

Q: Does the voice agent merge the backend into the same process?
A: No. The LiveKit voice agent and the RAG backend stay as separate services so they are easier to maintain, test, and scale.
